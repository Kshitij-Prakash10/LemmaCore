Limits

Every loco parameter is an assumption until a mentor confirms it. The baseline is a modelling assumption: no load shedding, with the loco's own protection derating and tripping. A stopped train does not count as unsafe. The advisor stops when the cautious (5th percentile) minutes left do not cover the trip, so some stops are not needed; that count is shown above. This is a simulation of our model, not of a real loco.
